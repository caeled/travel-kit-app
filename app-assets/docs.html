TRAVEL KIT · DOCUMENTATION

How the Travel Kit works

The Travel Kit packs five travel rooms from Steven Powell's Everyday Cool collection into one installable app: the sky above you tonight, the things you need wherever you are, the weather anywhere on Earth, radio from everywhere, and the fieldcraft that puts it all together outdoors.

Getting around

The home page is the front door: each of the five rooms has a card there. Links that stay inside the kit open here in the app. Links that leave it — data sources, live services, directions — open in your default browser, so the kit stays exactly where you left it.

The built-in Help page documents each room in depth: what it does, how to use it, and where its information comes from. Find It, The Radio Room, and The Survival Guide also carry Help menus of their own, and every room's full README rides along inside the app.

The five rooms

  • Tonight's Sky — a drag-and-zoom night sky, planets, the Moon's phase, satellite pass predictions, and radio astronomy explainers. Works offline.
  • Find It — set a place, tap one big category button, and get the nearest matches on a live OpenStreetMap map, each with directions. Needs the internet.
  • Weather Everywhere — a portable world-weather workshop: maps, comparisons, ocean and cloud labs, field missions, and live forecasts. The core workshop works offline.
  • The Radio Room — a retro wooden radio that really plays (six SomaFM stations on a make-believe FM dial), a shelf of everywhere radio lives now, and a scanner tuner that finds your county's feeds on Broadcastify. Needs the internet.
  • The Survival Guide — putting it all together outdoors: fire, water, soap, direction from the stars, a remedies shelf, and first aid, every card carrying a safety line and an honesty line. Works offline; only its links out need the internet.

The menu bar

  • File → Exit (on macOS, the Travel Kit menu → Quit) closes the app.
  • Help → Documentation opens this page.
  • Help → Product Index opens the index of Steven Powell's products — Everyday Cool, Spectrum Search, the Travel Kit, and Steven Powell Tech Services — in your browser.
  • Help → About Travel Kit shows the app name and version.

Online and offline

Tonight's Sky works fully offline, Weather Everywhere's core workshop does too, and so does The Survival Guide — only the forecasts and research links in Weather Everywhere and the Links Out shelf in the Guide need the internet. Find It needs the internet for its map, searches, and directions, and The Radio Room needs it for everything it plays or looks up: radio is live by nature. Each room's Help section says plainly where its data comes from and how fresh it is.

The family

The Travel Kit is one of Steven Powell's products, drawn from the Everyday Cool collection, which lives on the web at caeled.github.io/everyday-cool. The rooms here are the collection's own pages, not copies of copies.
